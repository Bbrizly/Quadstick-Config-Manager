QuadStick Config Manager Support

Help with editing, checking, importing, and exporting QuadStick profiles on iPhone and iPad.

Get help

Open a support issue on GitHub. Include the app version, your iOS version, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Remove personal information before sharing screenshots or profile files; profiles may contain your control mappings.

Common questions

  • How do I start? The app opens with a sample profile. Tap a device part to inspect its current assignment, or open Modes, Review Controls, and Profiles from the home screen.
  • How do I import or share a profile? Open a CSV file from Files, or import a public Google Sheets link. The app does not need Google sign-in to import a public sheet.
  • How do I save a profile? Choose Install to QuadStick and save the CSV in Files. To put it on the controller, choose the QuadStick drive if it appears in Files. iOS needs a compatible USB-C connection for direct drive access.
  • Why is Google Drive unavailable? Drive backup and sharing appear only in builds configured for Google sign-in. Use CSV export or public Sheets import when those controls are unavailable.
  • Does a tip unlock features? No. Editing, validation, import, and export are free; optional tips unlock nothing.

Privacy

Read the iOS privacy policy for local profile storage, optional Google Drive features in configured builds, and tip purchase data.
