SipStudio for iPhone: Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

SipStudio has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. Your profiles stay on your iPhone and in the places you save them with the Files app.

What uses the network

Importing from Google Sheets. When you paste a Sheets link and import it, the app downloads that one sheet from Google. Nothing is sent with it.

The tip jar. Tip prices and purchases go through Apple, and RevenueCat records them for the developer. RevenueCat receives a random ID made by the app and the tip you bought. It never receives your name, your Apple ID or your profiles. Tips unlock nothing.

What is never sent

Your profiles and their contents, file names, anything you type, and anything about who you are.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue.
