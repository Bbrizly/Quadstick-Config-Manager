# Joystick directions first

- Up, down, left and right now lead the joystick page's list of free inputs, ahead of the outer and inner ring. Thanks to Drew Redepenning.
- In the output list, the thumbsticks sit above the D-pad and read up, down, left, right.
- The plus on the joystick page starts on the next free direction, so each press adds a new box instead of another line under Up.
