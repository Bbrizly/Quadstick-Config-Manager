# Follow a Google Sheet, and send a profile over the console

- A profile imported from a Google Sheets link remembers the link once saved. "Get the latest from the link" downloads it again, shows what changed, and replaces your copy only after you say yes. Your old copy goes to the rescue folder first.
- The app also checks a linked sheet when you open the profile, when you come back to the window, and every 5 minutes while it is in front. A change shows a banner with a button to review it. Nothing is replaced on its own. Turn this off in Settings.
- The sheet is checked the same way an import is. A sheet with errors cannot replace your copy, and a sheet with fewer modes than your copy says so.
- A sheet that is not shared now says how to share it: Share, General access, Anyone with the link, Viewer.
- Google being busy no longer fails the download at once: it is tried again twice.
- Send a profile and measure sip and puff over the Bluetooth or cable console.
- On a Mac, games built on SDL can see the QuadStick as a gamepad in emulation mode 0, and the device page points to mode 7 for other games.
- The Mac App Store version can find the QuadStick drive once you choose it.
- Xbox controller bridge: a failed write to the virtual controller lets every button go and tries again, and a stopped bridge no longer hides an "unsupported" message.
- The warning for a mode past the device's 16 now says what happens: the device still lands on it when you switch modes, but none of its controls are read.
- Side tube on new modes and the printable reference sheet came in 1.8.6.
- Usage data, if you turned it on, now also carries the setup time and the library game, and the strength test result. See the privacy policy.
