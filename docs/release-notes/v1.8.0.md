Everything since 1.7.1. Most of it came from Drew Redepenning's September
review, who sets QuadSticks up for patients.

## Settings

- Joystick full deflection is now Joystick sensitivity.
- Hard sip/puff threshold says normal, which is the one ordinary sip and puff inputs use.
- Each group shows the few settings people change and folds the rest behind More options.
- The fold opens itself when your file already carries a folded setting.
- Bluetooth dropdowns say what each mode does instead of `game_pad`, `ssp` and `dtr`.
- Pairing modes read as words, not a bare 0, 1, 2 and 4.
- Plainer words on the output functions.

## Emulation modes

- All eight are on the list. The four that hide the drive say so on the label.
- `default.csv` still refuses to be written with a drive-hiding mode.
- One dropdown instead of nine stacked buttons.
- Adding a Preferences sheet asks which controller the file should make.

## The device picture

- The FPS and the Original have their own photos. The FPS was a copy of the classic one.
- All five hole pairings are labelled on the device and clickable.
- Picking one rings the holes it uses, joins them, and fades the rest.
- Switch jacks and USB devices put the back of the case on the stage.
- A Main controls row is the way back to the front.
- A part's mappings read in gesture order, not file order.
- The plus that adds a mapping sits in the part heading, beside the count.
- The Singleton's mode lights sit on its lenses.

## Importing

- Repeated warnings fold into one line with a count. Fifty blank rows raised fifty.
- Warnings about an unknown input stay one per cell.
- The review no longer runs off the right edge.
- A sheet headed "XBox Outputs" no longer hides outputs your rows already use.

## Fixes

- The add-mapping plus is back above the 48 pixel click-target floor.
- Eight strings were English in all twelve languages, including the import review's folded warning line.
- Every screenshot in the docs caught the settings fold mid-animation.

## Languages

- All of the above, in thirteen languages.
- The five hole pairing names were English built into the code.

## Known

- Live input, and the row lighting built on it, are proved against the firmware's
  own USB descriptors, not against hardware in a room. If the dot does not follow
  your stick, or a row lights for the wrong output, report that first.
