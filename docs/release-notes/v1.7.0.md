The biggest release since the app started. Everything below is new since 1.6.2.

## Your QuadStick's own settings, on their own screen

Device is one of the three places the app goes now. It reads `prefs.csv` off
the stick and gives every setting a real control: a slider with the exact
number beside it where the setting has a range, an on/off box where it is a
flag, a dropdown where it is a list. Every one says what it does in a line, and
what the device falls back to when your file does not carry it.

Nothing writes to the stick until you press Save, nothing you did not type is
rewritten, and the settings you have not changed are not touched.

There is also a live reading: plug the QuadStick in, open the page, and the dot
on the photo follows your stick while you tune it.

## The app watches your mapping while you use it

Plug a QuadStick in and the app says so on its own. You do not press anything.

While the stick sends an output, the row that sends it lights up in the editor.
Sip on the mouthpiece and the row bound to sip lights while the sip lasts, then
goes out. It is how you check a binding does what you meant without opening a
game.

## Read in thirteen languages

English, Arabic, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Italian, Japanese, Korean,
Dutch, Polish, Portuguese and Simplified Chinese. Input, output and function
names stay in English, because those are the bytes the device reads.

## The back of the QuadStick

Switch jacks are labelled by the socket they are in. The rear joystick and the
USB-A port are on the device view before anything is mapped to them.

## A look that holds together

New icon set, a real type and spacing scale, a calmer dark theme, and every
page built out of the same panels. Every click target meets a 48 pixel floor.

## Smaller things

- Emulation modes are picked by console name, and a mode that would hide the
  drive is blocked in `default.csv` rather than warned about.
- A mode can be set to USB or Bluetooth on its own.
- Every number a function takes says what it is, what it is measured in, and
  what the device substitutes when it is left out.
- The community list is searchable, and the output picker replaced a 400 item
  menu.
- Google Drive backup and Google Sheets import.
- A name your QuadStick cannot display is now warned about before you install.

## Known

Live input, and the row lighting built on it, have been proved against the
firmware's own USB descriptors, not against hardware in a room. If the dot does
not follow your stick, or a row lights for the wrong output, that is the thing
to report first.
